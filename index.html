About Apollo

Lighting is direction

Apollo Lighting is a premier gaffer and lighting design studio specialising in cinematic productions across film, television, commercials, and music videos.

Founded on the belief that great lighting is inseparable from great storytelling, we work closely with directors and cinematographers to sculpt the mood, atmosphere, and emotional truth of every frame.

From intimate indie films to large-scale productions, our team brings technical mastery and artistic intuition to every set.

How We Work

The Process

Discovery
We begin by understanding the director's visual language, the script's emotional arc, and the story we're trying to tell together.
Design
Detailed lighting plans, equipment lists, and diagrams are crafted to map every key, fill, and practical to the demands of each location and scene.
Execution
On set, our team works with precision and calm — rigging, balancing, and responding in real time to the flow of the shoot.
Refinement
We stay close through post, consulting on colour grading to ensure the light we captured translates faithfully to the final image.
